    How to Play

    Basics

    • Move with WASD / arrow keys, a gamepad stick, touch-drag, or by holding the mouse.
    • Your weapons attack automatically. Focus on positioning.
    • Collect experience gems. XP is shared by the team. Every level-up, everyone picks an upgrade.
    • You can carry 6 weapons and 6 passive items.
    • Survive until the timer ends to win. Bosses appear along the way.
    • If you fall, a teammate can revive you by standing next to your grave for 3 seconds.
    • Break braziers for food, magnets, gold and more.
    • Esc / P pauses for everyone · Enter opens chat · Tab switches spectator camera.

    Evolutions

    Get a weapon to max level, own its paired passive item, then open a chest (dropped by elites and bosses).
